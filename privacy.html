Polis World

Privacy policy · for the iPhone and iPad app and the game on this website · November 2026

The short version

Polis World collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game never sends your information to us. The one thing that can leave your device is optional: scores and achievements for Apple's Game Center, if you're signed in to it (see below).

What stays on your device

Your world, your progress and your settings are saved only on the device you play on. We can't see them, and apart from the optional Game Center scores below, they are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app (or clearing this website's data in your browser) deletes them.

Photos

If you choose to save a postcard from the game, the iPhone or iPad asks your permission first, and the picture goes only into your own photo library.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad, optional)

From version 1.1, if you're signed in to Apple's Game Center on your iPhone or iPad, the app sends your Quiz Showdown scores, winning race times, star count, the size of your in-game purse and the achievements you earn to Game Center, so they can appear on leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive your name or any other details about you. If you aren't signed in, nothing is sent. Parents can turn Game Center off, or limit it, in Screen Time on the device.

Playing together (website only)

On the website, playing with a friend connects your two browsers directly. A free public service (PeerJS) introduces the two computers to each other; nothing about you or your world is kept there. The iPhone and iPad app doesn't have this feature.

Children

Polis World was made for a seven-year-old and is safe for children: because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Game Center, if a family uses it, follows Apple's own rules for children's accounts.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the game does anything differently.
