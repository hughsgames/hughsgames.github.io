The World

Gold labels are places you've visited: click one to travel there. Grey ones you haven't reached yet; walk there first. Blue is your camp. The red dot is you, and green dots are animals you've tamed.

Your designs

Design a building by dragging walls, roofs, columns and more, or save something you already built by marking two opposite corners. Then build copies anywhere, turned whichever way you want. Copies use blocks from your bag.
