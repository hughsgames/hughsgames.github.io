Help and support · for the iPhone and iPad app

Get in touch

Questions, ideas or something not working? Email wknox5@gmail.com. Tell us which device you play on and what happened, and we'll answer as soon as we can.

Getting started

  • Walk with the joystick and drag on the screen to look around. Tap a block to dig it, or switch to building and tap to place one.
  • Walk up to someone and tap the blue Talk button to chat. Every wonder has a guide who tells you its story.
  • Open the Journal for your quests, the map (travel to any wonder you've found), your town and your settings.
  • Your purse sits under your stars. Each city uses its own money: sell things to the townspeople, work for the ruler's clerk (the one with the purple crown), and change money at the golden-domed World Bank south of Babylon.

Your world

Your world is saved on your iPhone or iPad as you play, and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes it, so keep the app if you want to keep your world.

Privacy

Polis World collects nothing about you. Read the privacy policy.
